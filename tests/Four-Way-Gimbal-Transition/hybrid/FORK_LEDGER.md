## New local rocker fork and blend

- **A, λ=0:** reference linear torque `−kθ` and potential `kθ²/2`.
- **B, λ=1:** reference smooth transition `−kθ[1+1.5 S(|θ|)]` with matching potential. B already equals A at and below `|θ|=0.10`; above `0.24` its torque is again linear, at 2.5 times the A slope. The nonlinearity is the smooth intervening response, not a categorical difference at every angle.
- **Hybrid proposal, λ=0.25, 0.50, 0.75:** `Fλ=(1−λ)FA+λFB` and `Uλ=(1−λ)UA+λUB`. Each λ is fixed for its run and the force remains the negative energy gradient. The weight is a candidate design parameter, not a probability, physical mixture measurement, or time-varying switch.
- **Status:** derived consequences of a stated toy-model proposal only. The exact endpoint equivalence, numerical checks, results and their limitations are documented in `RESULTS.md` and output files. No experiment determines whether any candidate describes nature.
- **To distinguish physically:** a specified physical geometry and independently measured torque–angle relation across both transition limits, with calibration uncertainties, external-work accounting and independent predictive tests. None is supplied here.
