## Preselected observables

For every weight report final-cycle sampled minimum and maximum rocker angle, RMS angle about zero (trapezoidal time integral), oscillation half-range, zero-crossing and turning counts, sampled angle-regime occupancy relative to B thresholds, 20-cycle actuator positive work, actuator negative-work magnitude, net work, passive damping loss, stored mechanical energy change, maximum absolute integrated energy residual, maximum state difference against tighter integration, gross-ledger component difference against tighter integration, and paired perturbation distance at cycles 1, 5 and 20 plus maximum transient distance. Preserve each actuator's positive and negative contributions. Report both the reference perturbation and its sign reverse. Stage-by-stage records cover all 80 quarters of the drive and all λ values. Final-cycle threshold occupancy uses 240 distinct equispaced samples; phase-window records use the reference's 61-point endpoint-inclusive convention.

Values are dimensionless synthetic model units. Occupancies and extrema are sample approximations; zero and velocity sign crossings use linear interpolation and may miss tangencies. RMS is not oscillation amplitude; it includes mean offset. 20-cycle work is not electrical input or efficiency. Mechanical-state norms mix coordinates and velocities and are not calibrated physical distances.

## Checks and acceptance thresholds (not statistical confidence bounds)

- Match reference at endpoints in force, energy, vector field, and complete 20-cycle trajectories; exact bitwise equality is expected under the same deterministic environment.
- Independently finite-difference the complete eight-coordinate potential and check every conservative-force component at each λ, including both transition boundaries and negative angles; independently check instantaneous `dH/dt = sum(Wdot+)−sum(Wdot−)−Qdot`.
- Check monotonicity of cumulative positive/negative work and passive loss, energy balance, finite values and exact trajectory shape `(4801,25)`. At common samples require max absolute ledger residual < `1e-7`, max absolute normal-vs-tight physical-state component difference < `5e-7`, max gross-ledger component difference < `1e-4`; independently record actual differences. Kinks in the work integrands can make gross-work discrepancies much larger than the canceled net energy residual.
- Check reference perturbation pairing and states; add the reverse pair without presenting either as a basin or stability survey. Compare the original pair with its tighter run.
- Round-trip every saved numeric archive, validate keys, shapes, time, monotonicity and finiteness, verify CSV counts, and compute a SHA-256 inventory for generated files. Hashes are an independent local integrity choice, not a claimed project manifest standard.
- No superiority criterion is declared: explicitly report when performance ordering differs across selected metrics. No claims of probability, self-powered oscillation, physical validation, general stability or real-world performance.

## Post-run diagnostic amendment (2026-11-20 18:56 UTC)

The initial full calculation (`run_initial.log`) exposed tiny negative increments in sampled gross-work ledger values even though the defined instantaneous work rates are nonnegative. A strict `1e-9` increment threshold failed. The original reference endpoint itself exhibits the effect; DOP853 dense-output interpolation around power sign changes is a plausible numerical mechanism, not a proof of its exact origin. Rather than discard the already computed trajectories, silently change them, or loosen the acceptance threshold and call it a pass, the output verifier now records each run's minimum increment, its index and ledger column, and the count of decreases exceeding `1e-9`; it reports the gross-work monotonicity check as failed if applicable. Passive-loss monotonicity is reported separately. The simulation is repeated with **unchanged** reference and tighter settings, and saved values are not altered. Mathematical/unit and file-integrity tests verify truthful diagnostics, not the failed claim of strict gross-work monotonicity. This reporting change was made after the first calculation and does not change the model, λ values, inputs, metrics, or original numerical acceptance thresholds.
